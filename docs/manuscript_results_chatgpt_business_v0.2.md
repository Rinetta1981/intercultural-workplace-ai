# Manuscript Results — ChatGPT Business Arm v0.2

## Intercultural Workplace AI

The frozen ChatGPT Business arm comprised 120 observations from 10 scenario families, four sociopragmatic conditions (DC, DI, MC, MI), and three replicates per family × condition cell. Analyses treated the scenario family as the experimental unit.

### Confirmatory outcomes

No confirmatory IWA test survived Holm correction across the nine preregistered primary tests. For competence, the mean directness contrast (Direct − Mitigated) was -0.133 (exact sign-flip p = .03125; Holm-adjusted p = .250). Leadership potential showed a larger negative directness contrast of -0.283 (exact p = .015625; Holm-adjusted p = .140625). Managerial-intervention need was completely invariant to directness, register, and their interaction (all mean contrasts = 0; all adjusted p = 1.000).

### Secondary outcomes

Directness produced significant negative effects on two secondary interpersonal-evaluation outcomes after BH-FDR correction. Professionalism decreased by 0.367 points on average under direct relative to mitigated wording (exact p = .0078125; BH-FDR p = .03515625), while cooperativeness decreased by 0.900 points (exact p = .001953125; BH-FDR p = .017578125). Communicative appropriateness also showed a negative directness contrast (-0.283), but this did not survive multiplicity correction (exact p = .125; BH-FDR p = .28125). No register or directness × register effect survived correction.

### Categorical managerial recommendations

The discrete managerial recommendation was almost invariant to directness. Under direct wording, the model returned `acknowledge` in 17/60 cases (28.3%), `clarify` in 8/60 (13.3%), and `none` in 35/60 (58.3%). Under mitigated wording, the corresponding counts were 17/60 (28.3%), 7/60 (11.7%), and 36/60 (60.0%). Register produced a somewhat larger descriptive redistribution from `acknowledge` toward `clarify`, but the dominant `none` recommendation remained essentially unchanged. These categorical outcomes were designated descriptive/exploratory in the analysis plan.

### Role-relation exploration

The directness penalty was descriptively strongest in peer scenarios. Mean directness contrasts for cooperativeness were -0.611 in downward, -1.222 in peer, and -0.875 in upward scenarios. The corresponding professionalism contrasts were -0.111, -0.611, and -0.375. Because role relation was not independently crossed within each scenario family, these subgroup differences are descriptive and should not be interpreted as causal moderation by hierarchy.

### Ordinal-model sensitivity

The preregistered cumulative-link mixed-model sensitivity analysis broadly supported the directness effect on interpersonal evaluation. Clean ordinal fits showed negative directness coefficients for cooperativeness, professionalism, leadership potential, and communicative appropriateness. The competence model was numerically unstable and was not interpreted. Managerial-intervention need was not estimable because the outcome was constant across all 120 observations.

### Interpretation

Across IWA outcomes, the most robust pattern was a directness penalty in interpersonal person perception, especially cooperativeness and professionalism, alongside substantial robustness in downstream managerial-action judgments. Because the stimuli contained no demographic, national, ethnic, or cultural-group identities, these results should be interpreted as sociopragmatic sensitivity to communication style rather than as evidence of demographic or national-group bias.
